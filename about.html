About the Global Ethanol Association

Driving the Future of Sustainable Energy Worldwide

The Global Ethanol Association (GEA) is the international voice of the ethanol industry — uniting producers, technology providers, investors, and industry leaders to accelerate the adoption of ethanol as a clean, scalable, and commercially viable energy solution across all sectors.

Founded by Industry Leaders Built on decades of expertise in energy, finance, and maritime industries
Who We Are

A Global Platform for Ethanol Excellence

The Global Ethanol Association was founded with a clear conviction: ethanol is one of the most practical, scalable, and immediately deployable solutions to the world's decarbonisation challenges. We operate at the intersection of energy, finance, technology, and policy — bringing together the right stakeholders to make ethanol adoption a reality.

From marine fuels to industrial applications, from project origination to commercial structuring, GEA serves as the bridge between ambition and execution. Our members span the entire value chain — producers, shippers, technology innovators, investors, and end-users — all united by a shared commitment to a lower-emission future.

  • International infrastructure & energy transition project development
  • Maritime decarbonisation & sustainable marine fuel advocacy
  • Strategic partnerships connecting capital with industry
  • Market development across the entire ethanol value chain

Vision & Mission

What Drives Us Every Day

Our vision and mission are anchored in the belief that ethanol can transform how the world powers its future — and that demand drives change.

Our Vision

A World Powered by Clean, Scalable Ethanol

We envision a future where ethanol is recognised and adopted globally as a primary driver of decarbonisation — powering ships, industries, and communities with a fuel that is renewable, practical, and economically viable. We see a world where the transition to lower-emission energy is not aspirational but operational, and where ethanol stands at the forefront of that transformation.

Our Mission

Accelerating Ethanol Adoption Through Collaboration

Our mission is to unite the global ethanol ecosystem — producers, technology providers, investors, policymakers, and end-users — to accelerate the development, commercialisation, and adoption of ethanol across all applications. We work to remove barriers, create market demand, facilitate strategic partnerships, and turn ethanol's potential into real-world impact. Guided by the principle that demand drives change, we convert market interest into concrete projects, commercial partnerships, and practical fuel supply solutions.

Our Journey

The Road So Far

From a bold idea to a global movement — here's how GEA has grown and evolved.

Foundation

GEA is Founded

Sylvain Zurcher and Morten Jacobsen co-found the Global Ethanol Association with a shared vision: to unite the global ethanol ecosystem and accelerate its adoption as a clean, scalable energy solution. The founding principle — "Demand Drives Change" — becomes the association's guiding philosophy.

Maritime Focus

Championing Ethanol as a Marine Fuel

GEA begins its focused advocacy for ethanol as a sustainable marine fuel — engaging with shipowners, port authorities, and fuel suppliers to demonstrate ethanol's viability as a scalable, lower-emission alternative for global shipping.

Strategic Growth

Building the Board & Expanding Membership

GEA welcomes distinguished industry leaders to its Board of Directors — including representatives from WinGD, Berkel AHK, VALE, Edeniq, and SQ GROUP — bringing world-class expertise in engineering, energy, technology, and international trade.

Ecosystem Development

Growing the Global Network

The association expands its reach across the entire ethanol value chain — forging partnerships with producers, technology providers, financial institutions, and end-users worldwide. GEA becomes a recognised platform for connecting capital with industry.

Looking Ahead

Scaling for Global Impact

Today, GEA is focused on converting growing market interest into concrete projects and commercial partnerships. With an expanding membership base and an unwavering commitment to its mission, the association is poised to play a defining role in the global energy transition.
