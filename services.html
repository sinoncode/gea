Who can use GEA services?

Services are designed for members and partners across the value chain. Contact us to discuss eligibility for your organisation.

Can services be combined?

Yes. Many engagements combine several services, for example supply chain development together with partnership matchmaking.

It depends on scope. Each service page lists a typical timeframe, and we confirm a plan after an initial conversation.
