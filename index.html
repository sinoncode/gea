About GEA

Uniting the global ethanol industry for a cleaner energy future

The Global Ethanol Association brings together producers, technology providers, researchers and partners to advance renewable fuels, support innovation and strengthen collaboration across the energy transition.

  • Representing the ethanol value chain at international level
  • Evidence-based policy dialogue and advocacy
  • Shared research, standards and best practice

Direction

Our Vision & Our Mission

The principles that guide GEA and its members worldwide.

Our Vision

A world powered by sustainable, low-carbon fuels

A global energy system in which renewable ethanol plays a recognised, trusted role in decarbonising transport and strengthening energy security.

Our Mission

Connect, represent and advance the ethanol industry

To unite the global industry, drive innovation and sustainability, and promote collaboration that delivers clean energy solutions for a better tomorrow.
