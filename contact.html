Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about GEA, membership, and our work in the ethanol industry.

The Global Ethanol Association is an international industry association dedicated to accelerating the development, commercialisation, and adoption of ethanol as a clean, scalable, and commercially viable energy solution. We unite producers, technology providers, investors, and end-users across the entire value chain — with a particular focus on maritime decarbonisation and sustainable marine fuels.

GEA membership is open to organisations and individuals across the ethanol value chain — including producers, distributors, technology and equipment providers, shipowners, port authorities, financial institutions, research institutions, and industry consultants. We welcome both established companies and innovative startups committed to advancing ethanol adoption.

Members gain access to a curated global network of industry leaders, exclusive market intelligence and research, participation in working groups and sector initiatives, speaking opportunities at industry events, and direct connections to potential partners, investors, and customers. GEA also advocates on behalf of members with policymakers and regulatory bodies worldwide.

GEA works at the forefront of advancing ethanol as a marine fuel. We engage with shipowners, engine manufacturers, port authorities, and fuel suppliers to demonstrate ethanol's viability as a scalable, lower-emission alternative. Our work includes market development, fuel supply chain development, strategic partnerships, and stakeholder engagement — guided by our principle that "Demand Drives Change."

GEA originates, structures, and develops international infrastructure and energy transition projects — from initial concept through financing to delivery. This includes ethanol production facilities, fuel supply chain infrastructure, maritime refuelling capabilities, and integrated renewable energy projects. We bring together investors, lenders, industrial companies, and technology providers to make these projects commercially viable and execution-ready.

To explore a project partnership, simply reach out through our contact form or email us directly. Our team will schedule an introductory call to understand your objectives, assess alignment with GEA's mission, and identify the most appropriate form of collaboration — whether that's project origination, commercial structuring, market development, or strategic introductions within our network.

Yes. GEA organises and participates in industry events, roundtables, webinars, and conferences worldwide. These events provide platforms for knowledge sharing, networking, and showcasing innovations in ethanol production and application. Members receive priority invitations and discounted registration. Check our website regularly for upcoming events or subscribe to our newsletter.

GEA is committed to promoting ethanol as a genuinely sustainable energy solution. We advocate for responsible production practices, lifecycle emissions reduction, and the use of advanced feedstocks and production technologies. Our goal is to ensure that ethanol's growth contributes meaningfully to global decarbonisation targets while supporting economic development and energy security.

You can stay informed by subscribing to our newsletter, following us on LinkedIn and other social media channels, and regularly visiting our website. Members also receive exclusive briefings, market reports, and early access to research publications and event invitations.

Membership applications are typically reviewed within 10–15 business days. Once your application is submitted, our membership team will reach out to schedule an introductory conversation. Upon approval, you'll receive a welcome package with your membership credentials, access details, and information on how to maximise the value of your GEA membership.

The Global Ethanol Association is headquartered in Cortaillod, Switzerland (Chemin du Vivier 6, CH-2016 Cortaillod). Our organisation is completely based in Switzerland, serving our international membership and partners across the global energy and maritime sectors.
